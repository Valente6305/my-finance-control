Transaction handling

Balances are displayed separately by account. We never add multiple balance types for one account, and do not automatically overwrite your CGD budget baseline or protected emergency reserve.

Imported transactions have stable IDs for duplicate detection. Transactions categorised as transfers must be excluded from spending. Please check categorisation manually, as automatic detection is not yet reliable.

Privacy and status

This application runs from public GitHub Pages. Your imported financial files are handled inside your iPhone browser and are not uploaded to GitHub by this page. The Mac's daily synchronisation does not automatically reach the iPhone; transfer a new encrypted snapshot when you wish to update the phone.
