Transaction handling

CGD current-account cash and CGD credit-card figures are separate. Confirm each account type once; the mapping will be reused for later snapshots. Credit-card available credit is not cash and is not necessarily the amount owed.

New movements are categorised conservatively by their descriptions. Matched CGD–Revolut transfers are excluded from spending; repayments are separate from card purchases. Review uncategorised movements and confirm values from your bank statements.

Privacy and status

This application runs from public GitHub Pages. Your imported financial files are handled inside your iPhone browser and are not uploaded to GitHub by this page. The Mac's daily synchronisation does not automatically reach the iPhone; transfer a new encrypted snapshot when you wish to update the phone.
